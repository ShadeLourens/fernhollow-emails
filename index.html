Fernhollow emails

Emails for Fernhollow Animal Alliance, a fictional animal charity. Written in MJML and built for Engaging Networks, to go with the Second Chances donation page.

Each email has two versions: one with Engaging Networks merge fields, ready to paste into the platform, and a preview with sample data for test sends.

1. The appeal

  • Engaging Networks version of the appeal (shows the raw merge fields)
  • Plain-text preview and Engaging Networks plain text
  • In the browser: 600px, 320px phone and images turned off

Test sends in real inboxes:

  • Gmail: on the web, phone app and phone app in dark mode
  • Apple Mail: on a Mac, iPhone and iPhone in dark mode
  • Outlook.com: light and dark mode
  • Images turned off: Apple Mail and Outlook.com

About the unsubscribe link

This is a demo, so there's no mailing list to leave. In Engaging Networks, this link opens the subscription management page, where people can change which emails they get or unsubscribe.

Fernhollow Animal Alliance is a fictional organization created for a portfolio. Biscuit's photos are AI-generated, and no donations are processed.
