Fernhollow emails

Emails for Fernhollow Animal Alliance, a fictional animal charity. Written in MJML and built for Engaging Networks, to go with the Second Chances donation page.

Each email has two versions: one with Engaging Networks merge fields, ready to paste into the platform, and a preview with sample data for test sends.
